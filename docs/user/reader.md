# Reader View

The integrated reader in NH Reader is engineered for maximum performance, reading comfort, and responsiveness across displays of all sizes.

---

## Accessing the Reader

- From any gallery details view, click **Read Now** to begin from page 1.
- Alternatively, click any thumbnail in the gallery's thumbnail grid to open the reader directly at that page.

---

## Layout and Boundaries

The reader view operates in an unobstructed, full-window layout:
- **Title Bar**: The system title bar and top header remain visible at the top, allowing easy window dragging, minimizing, or navigation back to the gallery details page.
- **Hidden Dock**: The bottom navigation dock automatically hides during active reading sessions to maximize vertical screen real estate.
- **Page Containment**: Pages are positioned below the header, ensuring content is never clipped or obscured by overlay gradients.

---

## Reading Modes

You can switch reading modes using the mode toggle in the reader control bar or through keyboard shortcuts:

1. **Single Page**:
   - Displays one page centered at a time.
   - Ideal for portrait monitors, laptops, and detailed artwork inspection.
2. **Double Page (Manga Spread)**:
   - Displays two facing pages side by side.
   - Supports Right-to-Left (RTL, default for manga) or Left-to-Right (LTR) reading directions.
   - Automatically handles the cover page as a standalone single page if configured.
3. **Continuous Vertical (Webtoon)**:
   - Pages are stacked vertically in a smooth continuous scroll stream.
   - Perfect for high-refresh-rate monitors and wheel-based navigation.

---

## Fit & Scaling Options

- **Fit Width**: Scales the image to span the full width of the viewing viewport.
- **Fit Height**: Scales the image so the entire height fits within the available vertical space without scrolling.
- **Fit Both (Best Fit)**: Scales the image proportionately so the entire page is visible without any scrollbars.
- **Original Size (1:1)**: Renders the image at its native resolution.

---

## Navigation & Controls

### Keyboard Shortcuts

| Action | Primary Shortcut | Secondary Shortcut |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Next Page** | `Right Arrow` | `D` or `Page Down` |
| **Previous Page** | `Left Arrow` | `A` or `Page Up` |
| **First Page** | `Home` | — |
| **Last Page** | `End` | — |
| **Toggle Fullscreen** | `F11` | `F` |
| **Exit Reader / Back** | `Escape` | `Backspace` |
| **Zoom In** | `+` or `=` | `Ctrl + Wheel Up` |
| **Zoom Out** | `-` | `Ctrl + Wheel Down` |
| **Reset Zoom** | `0` | — |

### Mouse & Touch Navigation
- Click the right half of the image to advance; click the left half to go back.
- Move the cursor near the top or bottom of the window to reveal the reader toolbar and page slider.
- Use the scrubber slider at the bottom of the reader bar to jump instantly to any page in the book.
